The Foundation

1. Problem & Solution

Why StartupLabs exists and what problem it aims to solve

The Problem

Startup founders often struggle to find people with the right skills to turn their ideas into reality. At the same time, students may have technical, creative, business, or marketing skills but limited opportunities to apply them to real-world projects.

Founders and students can find it difficult to discover and connect with each other through one focused platform.

Our Solution

StartupLabs aims to connect startup founders and project owners with talented students from different colleges, branches, and academic backgrounds so they can collaborate on real-world projects.

Fostering cross-disciplinary execution across engineering, design, marketing, and business.
